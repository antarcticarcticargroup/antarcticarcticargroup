2025

Third AAARG! Workshop
Grenoble, France
May 5–8, 2025

The third AAARG! workshop was held at the Institut des Géosciences de l’Environnement (IGE), University Grenoble Alpes. This hybrid event focused on Antarctic ARs, with extended discussions on Arctic and Patagonian ARs, featuring presentations, thematic sessions, and a group hike in the surrounding mountains.

Participants: Luisa Emilia Aviles Podgurski, Victoire Buffet, James Butler, Niels Dutrievoz, Vincent Favier, Ricardo Fonseca, Irina Gorodetskaya, Kirstin Hoffmann-Abdi, Phoebe Jackson, Melanie Lauer, Gabrielle Linscott, Barthélemy Léonard, Sinéad McGetrick, Tuule Müürsepp, Christine Shields, Alexander Simon, Kazu Takahashi, Vijay Tallapragada, Sharath Chandra Thota, Sofie Tiedeck, Christian Torres, Andries-Jan De Vries, Jonathan D. Wille, Chen Zhang, Xun Zou

Organizing team: Vincent Favier & Victoire Buffet

2023

Second AAARG! Workshop
Boulder, CO, USA
June 27–29, 2023

This hybrid workshop included the launch of a review paper project (now published in Nature Reviews Earth & Environment), a field excursion into the Rocky Mountains, and an AR labeling session for machine learning development.

Participants: Cécile Agosta, Becca Baiman, James Booth, Victoire Buffet, Mathieu Casado, John Cassano, Liz Cassano, Kyle Clem, Francis Codron, Quentin Dalaiden, Tri Datta, Alice DuVivier, Devon Dunmire, Niels Dutrievoz, Vincent Favier, Elise Fourré, Irina Gorodetskaya, Adam Herrington, Matthew Lazzara, Gabrielle Linscott, Michelle MacLennan, Bradley Markle, Kyle Mattingly, Gerald Meehl, Benjamin Pohl, Hamish Prince, Tristan Rendfrey, Joel Savarino, Joseph Schnaubelt, David Schneider, Mark Seefeldt, Christine Shields, Aneesh Subramanian, Kateryna Tkachenko, Luke Trusel, Elina Valkonen, Étienne Vignon, Jonathan Wille, Andrew Winters, Benjamin Woods, Ziqi Yin, Chen Zhang, Zhenhai Zhang, Jerry Zou

Organizing team: Christine Shields, Andrew Winters, Rebecca Baiman, Michelle MacLennan, Vincent Favier & Tri Datta

2022

First AAARG! Workshop
Grenoble, France
June 27–28, 2022

The inaugural workshop established the AAARG! community and focused on identifying key challenges in understanding Antarctic atmospheric rivers. Held at IGE, University Grenoble Alpes, it brought together researchers for open discussion and future planning.

Participants: Cécile Agosta, Rebecca Baiman, Léonard Barthélemy, Juliette Blanchet, Quentin Dalaiden, Rajashree Datta, Claudio Durán-Alarcón, Vincent Favier, Irina Gorodetskaya, Christophe Leroy-Dos Santos, Michelle MacLennan, David Mikolajczyk, Benjamin Pohl, Christine Shields, Jonathan Wille

Organizing team: Vincent Favier & Jonathan Wille
